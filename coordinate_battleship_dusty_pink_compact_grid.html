⚓ Coordinate Battleship

Mission: Find and sink 3 bigger hidden ships (5, 4, and 3 points long)! Enter an ordered pair (x, y) to fire. x moves left/right; y moves up/down. The first number is always x. The x-axis goes from −5 to 5 and the y-axis goes from −2 to 4. After 5 misses in a row, you get a location hint!

You can also click a grid intersection to choose a coordinate, then press Fire!

Captain, choose your first coordinate!

How to play

Each ship lies on consecutive integer points along a horizontal or vertical line. A red X is a hit; a blue dot is a miss. Sink all ships to win. Repeated shots do not count. After every 5 consecutive misses, a hint reveals the region of one unhit ship point.
